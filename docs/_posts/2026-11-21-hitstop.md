## What I Worked On

Today I experimented with hitstop in Unity.

Hitstop briefly freezes gameplay when an attack
connects, helping attacks feel more impactful.

## My Approach

I used Time.timeScale to temporarily pause
gameplay and a coroutine to restore it.

## Challenges

Managing repeated hits without overlapping
hitstop effects.
